Using Anchor

Terms of use

Last updated: October 10, 2026

Anchor is a free student-built prototype that helps you organize benefit notices and follow through on the next steps. These terms explain what to expect when you use it.

What Anchor provides

Anchor offers document reading, a review workspace, task and deadline organization, progress and receipt tracking, exports, and optional account saving. The current notice-reading prototype focuses on English-language CalFresh renewal and verification notice families. Other documents may be unsupported.

Anchor is an independent project. It is not affiliated with a government agency and does not determine eligibility, award benefits, approve documents, or file applications. Recording a submission in Anchor records what you tell it happened; it does not send anything to an agency.

Check your original notice

Automated reading can miss text, misread checkboxes, or interpret a date incorrectly. Review extracted details against your original document before relying on a plan or calendar export. The issuing agency's requirements and decisions control your benefits.

Use official agency channels to confirm instructions, submit records, request an extension, or address a missed deadline. Anchor does not provide legal advice or guarantee that following its suggested steps will preserve benefits. Keep original notices and actual submission confirmations separately.

Your records and account

Use only documents you are authorized to access. Keep your sign-in and device secure, and do not try to access another person's workspace, bypass access controls, or upload harmful files. You retain your rights to the documents and information you add; Anchor uses them to provide the features you choose.

Guest records stay on the current device. Account records must successfully sync before another device can access them. Download backups when needed. See the privacy policy for storage, sign-in, providers, and deletion controls.

Availability and changes

As a prototype, Anchor may have errors, outages, storage limits, or changes to its features. Continuous availability, accurate extraction, complete records, and successful synchronization cannot be guaranteed. Calendar reminders require the calendar application you choose; Anchor does not send background notifications.

Updates to these terms will be published here with a new date. Questions about the project, errors, or your account can be sent to jaisamadhiya@gmail.com. Please keep sensitive benefit documents out of email.
