# Local coverage and reviewed sources

Checked 2026-10-11. The public catalog lives in `src/domain/district-services.ts`. County selection routes the resident to official resources; Anchor does not infer congressional district membership from an address, ZIP code, city, or county.

Sam Liccardo currently represents California's 16th Congressional District in the 119th Congress. The district includes parts of Santa Clara and San Mateo counties, as confirmed by the [Santa Clara Registrar of Voters](https://vote.santaclaracounty.gov/16th-congressional-district) and the [Census 119th Congress district map](https://www2.census.gov/geo/maps/cong_dist/cd119/cd_based/ST06/CD119_CA16.pdf). A county's benefit-service coverage is distinct from the district's boundaries.

California's Proposition 50 map governs the 2026 congressional election, while current representation continues under the existing lines until noon January 3, 2027. The [Secretary of State explains the effective dates](https://www.sos.ca.gov/elections/california-redistricting). The [Senate Office of Demographics map labeled 2025](https://sdmg.senate.ca.gov/committeehome/2025-congressional-districts/congressional-district-16-2025) is for the district seated in January 2027. Use the [official House representative lookup](https://www.house.gov/representatives/find-your-representative) to verify the current representative; that service explains the current-Congress versus 2026-election distinction. Resolve a split ZIP with the official service or congressional office rather than assigning the whole ZIP to one district.

Use the county and worker identified on the notice for case-specific questions. Check the current office directory before travel; an older map of San Mateo HSA offices lists locations that differ from its current office directory. The catalog deliberately links the current directory rather than duplicating every office address or opening hour.

CF 37 and SAR 7B are CDSS statewide forms, not congressional district forms. CDSS lists [CF 37 (11/16)](https://www.cdss.ca.gov/inforesources/forms-brochures/forms-alphabetic-list/a-d) and [SAR 7B / SAR 7AB (12/23)](https://www.cdss.ca.gov/inforesources/forms-brochures/forms-alphabetic-list/q-t). CF 37 concerns CalFresh recertification; SAR 7B is the blank eligibility status report and SAR 7AB supplies its instructions. SAR 7 has CalFresh and cash-aid sections with different applicability. Compare the blank with the county-supplied form and use the county's report period, notice and accepted submission instructions. A form revision is not proof that all work-rule or eligibility language remains current.

Anchor's source guidance covers its listed public-program topics. Guided official form preparation currently concerns CF 37 and SAR 7B; PDF export uses reviewed mappings into a verified blank and leaves signatures empty. The notice parser recognizes CF 377.2, CF 377.6 and explicit CalFresh interview appointments. Other programs' official links and general guidance do not imply supported notice extraction or a mapped official PDF. CalWORKs has county resource links; it is not a separate cloud-assistant program topic.

The form AI boundary accepts only reviewed identifiers: form, section, optional field and help type. Its trusted policy reconstructs public questions and guidance from the reviewed forms. No notice, highlighted PDF text, resident answer, draft, account details or arbitrary prompt can enter the provider request through `formContext`. New unreviewed field IDs fall back in the UI to the reviewed section; they are never accepted by the server as free text.
